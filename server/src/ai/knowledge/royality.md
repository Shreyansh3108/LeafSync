## Royalty & Payments Policy
- 80/20 royalty split: 80% of net profit per copy goes to the author, 20% to BookLeaf.
- Net profit = MRP minus printing cost, platform commission (Amazon/Flipkart), and shipping charges.
- Calculated quarterly and paid within 45 days of the quarter ending via bank transfer to the account linked in the author's dashboard.
- Minimum payout threshold: Rs 1,000. Balances below Rs 1,000 roll over to the next quarter.
- Missing data rule: Do not invent printing costs, per-platform sales, or bank-detail status. Ask the author to confirm their bank details are linked in the dashboard, and offer to share a detailed line-by-line royalty breakdown. If a payout is overdue, apologise and escalate to the finance team with a 48-hour resolution timeline.
