## Distribution & Availability Policy
- Books are listed on Amazon India, Flipkart, Amazon US, Amazon UK, and the BookLeaf Store.
- New listings go live within 7-10 business days after publication is complete.
- If a published book shows as "Currently Unavailable" or out of stock on a platform, it typically indicates a stock sync issue. BookLeaf's distribution team can trigger a re-sync, which resolves within 24-48 hours.

### Few-Shot Example
Author Query: "My book is published but it's showing as 'Currently Unavailable' on Amazon."
Response Guidance: Explain that this is typically a stock sync issue. Confirm that we are triggering a platform re-sync with the distribution team and set a clear expectation of 24-48 hours for the listing to go live again.
