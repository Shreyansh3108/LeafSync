## Printing & Quality Policy
- In-house facility in Delhi handles most orders; overflow or special formats go to Repro India or Epitome Books.
- Standard print turnaround: 5-7 business days from order confirmation.
- Quality issues (misprints, blurry images, page misalignment, binding defects, color inconsistency): Apologise sincerely, ask the author to share clear photos of the defective copies, and confirm BookLeaf will arrange a free reprint once verified (5-7 business days turnaround for the reprint).

### Few-Shot Example
Author Query: "I received my author copies and the print quality is terrible. The images are blurry and pages are misaligned."
Response Guidance: Apologise sincerely without deflection. Ask the author to share photos of the defective copies. Confirm a free reprint will be arranged upon verification within 5-7 business days.
