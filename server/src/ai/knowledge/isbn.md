## ISBN & Metadata Policy
- Every book published through BookLeaf receives a unique ISBN assigned under BookLeaf's publisher imprint. Authors wanting their own imprint must obtain an ISBN independently.
- Any ISBN error (duplicate ISBN, wrong book linked, mismatch between physical copy and online listing) is treated as a high-priority data issue and escalated immediately to the production team with a 48-hour resolution timeline.
- Metadata updates (such as book descriptions) can be submitted via the dashboard or email and reflect on platforms within 3-5 business days.

### Few-Shot Example
Author Query: "My book is showing a different ISBN on Amazon than what's on the physical copy."
Response Guidance: Acknowledge this as a serious data issue and apologise directly. Confirm immediate escalation to the production team and provide a 48-hour resolution timeline.
